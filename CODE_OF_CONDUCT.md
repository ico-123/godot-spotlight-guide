# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful, discuss ideas rather than people, assume good intent, give
actionable feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats or disclosure
of another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior makes the project unsafe.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their public
GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed with discretion.
